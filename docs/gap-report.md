# gmod-rust gap report: Garry's Mod (binary + SDK + Lua) vs the Rust rewrite

Compiled 2026-11-09 from the behaviour notes and gap lists of 13 reverse-engineering agents (one per subsystem) against `rep0rtDev/gmod-rust` `main` (clone of 2026-11-09). Every fact below is the agents' fact; the source and confidence column reproduces their tags (`bin` = pseudo-C of the user's own GMod binaries, read for understanding only; `SDK` = Source SDK 2013; `Lua` = Facepunch/garrysmod; `measured` = the repo's own srcds recordings; `INF` = inference). Where this report adds anything of its own it is marked **[inference, combine step]**. No pseudo-C is quoted and no original identifiers are kept; asset, convar and Lua hook names are used as-is.

Missing input: the `sound` gap list (`sound-gaps.md`) could not be downloaded (two attempts, storage error). The sound rows of the table are rebuilt from the sound agent's structured summary (8 ranked gaps, with Rust file refs) and from its behaviour note `sound.md`, and are marked "agent summary" in the source column. No agent failed outright; all 13 notes were read.

## 1. Executive summary

**Where the rewrite stands.** gmod-rust reproduces the *skeleton* of sandbox play with good fidelity where the repo measured things against srcds: tick-level timers and convar defaults of the physgun/gravgun/toolgun, the gravgun hold-point geometry, HL2 weapon cadences, clip sizes and spread values (except the pistol), movement speeds, footstep intervals and the 90/220 and 60/80 thresholds, the duck-crouch speed rule, the ground-impact 70 u/s and (v/320)^2 sound law, gravity, quadratic drag (within 0.1 %), the f1*f2 friction rule, rope behaviour, the HUD panel geometry (health 16,432 102x36, suit 140,432, ammo r150 136x36), death notices, pickup history, notifications, fog/skybox/water refraction, and the SENT think cadence (0.1 s then 0.2 s). The repo's `docs/spec` is largely right where it was measured.

**Where it is far from the game.** The agents filed **424 gaps** (50 P0 "obvious to any player", 169 P1 "noticeable", 205 P2 "subtle") across 13 subsystems. They fall into six classes:

1. **Effects and look are placeholders.** Explosions are one 96 u `zerogxplode` quad instead of 64 `fire_cloud2` fireballs + 44 smoke puffs + 16 embers + debris; every bullet impact shows an additive flash sprite the game never draws, while the game's per-material sparks/flecks/dust are absent; the muzzle flash is one tinted sprite for the local player only; the physgun beam is one 12 u cubic strip in a fixed colour instead of three random-width quadratic beams in the player's weapon colour with seven `physbeam` end sprites; the gravgun claws never open, its sprites are the wrong set and the hold loop never plays; the toolgun shows no `ToolTracer` and no selection dot/rings; no entity shadows exist at all; the flashlight is a cosine cone light rather than the projected `effects/flashlight001` texture; there is no weapon view punch on any weapon. (physgun, gravgun, toolgun, hl2weapons, clientfx, render)
2. **Three physics subsystems are missing entirely.** No VPhysics player shell (players cannot push props, platforms do not carry them), constraint `forcelimit`/`torquelimit` are stored but never break anything, and there is no buoyancy (props sink like in air). Velocity caps (2000 u/s, 3600 deg/s), the ~1.8 s sleep timing, the per-axis shadow-controller clamp, collision groups/`ShouldCollide`, and the collision-callback payload follow. Prop breaking leaves nothing behind (no gibs, no explosive props, no `propdata/*.txt`), map `prop_physics`/`prop_door_rotating`/logic entities are not spawned. (vphysics, props)
3. **The sound model is a different engine.** Distance attenuation is GoldSrc "linear to 1000/attn" instead of Source's 1/r beyond `36*10^((lvl-60)/20)` u; impact hard/soft is chosen by speed < 320 instead of the surfaceproperties hardness keys; there is no `dsp_presets.txt`/room DSP/soundscape DSP/soundmixers (Steam Audio reverb instead); footstep volume ignores the material; the world-sound record carries no channel/level/flags/dsp, so Lua `EmitSound` semantics and `SND_STOP`/`SND_CHANGE` are impossible. (sound)
4. **Lua compatibility breaks at the contract level.** Gamemode start-up hook order is wrong (`CreateTeams` must be first, `Initialize` after `PostGamemodeLoaded`), timers fire after `Tick` instead of between `Think` and `Tick`, `net` has no bit framing/ids/limits (tables as JSON, `ReadHeader` = 0, CRC ids, case-sensitive), legacy NWVars have no cadence/budget/truncation, `include`/`require`/`file` are stubs, connect hooks cannot reject, `OnLuaError` is late with no stack. (luabind, toolgun SENT bridge)
5. **Fitted constants stand in for the game's formulas.** Movement ground probe (1.2/1.0/0.4 u reaches instead of the +2/-1 u lower-half-hull sweep), StayOnGround, landing thresholds, noclip (SDK glide instead of direct velocity), duck 0.4 s with a hard eye switch (sandbox: 0.1 s with a 3v^2-2v^3 spline), missile guidance regression (game: 12.5 % blend toward the dot every 0.1 s), frag throw (1189/110/33.69 instead of eye+fwd*18+right*8 and (fwd+0.1 up)*1200 + player velocity), physgun controller magnitude clamp and `max_dv`, restitution floor 0.417+0.467f, weld as a 60 Hz spring, ragdoll friction scale 0.05, auto-exposure from a bright-pixel fraction, bloom threshold, light styles at their mean. (movement, hl2weapons, physgun, vphysics, render)
6. **Rust-only behaviours the game does not have** (remove): dark-red death fade, crowbar deployed on spawn (game: physgun / `cl_defaultweapon`), "fall deaths never respawn", idle physgun aim-beam, impact flash sprite, gravgun carry snapping to 90 deg, noclip acceleration/friction, engine-level jump boost for every player (game: sandbox Lua only), decals expiring at 120 s / 256 cap (game: 2048 ring, no expiry), orange dlight while holding with the gravgun, trigger "no flags = players", single-modifier prop damage, fonts scaled by screenH/480 (game: fixed 32/64/40 px), `ALT` secondary-ammo panel, `dim` bucket numbers, "Timer Failed" text, deterministic hook order.

**Confidence.** The binary dumps were partial for every agent: no `vphysics/` or `engine/` code for most, no client code for several, and float constants live in data sections the pseudo-C does not resolve. Numbers that could not be read were taken from SDK 2013 and tagged (`M`/`SDK`); the table keeps those tags. Open measured-vs-binary conflicts that one srcds recording would settle: sandbox duck time (0.1 vs 0.4 s), landing thresholds (350/290/580 vs 303/263.25/526.5), noclip constants, swim-sound period (500 vs 1000 ms), `+use` carry speed (see contradictions).

**Suggested first month.** Section 4 gives the full order; the short version is: (a) foundations that many gaps depend on — per-player movement values + duck spline, server view punch, networked weapon element state, `EffectEvent` payloads carrying magnitude/material letter, a `WorldSound` with channel/level/flags, fixed-px HUD fonts + a HudAnimations state machine, Lua hook order/timers; (b) the physics trio (player shell, breakable constraints, buoyancy) plus velocity caps; (c) the visible effect sets (explosion, impacts, muzzle flash, physgun beam, gravgun sprites/claws, tool tracer); (d) the Rust-only behaviours to delete, which are cheap and immediately visible.

## 5. Contradictions and inconsistencies between agents' notes

C1. **`+use` carry speed, 1000 vs 2500 u/s.** props.md #85 (`[sdk high]`, "binary not verifiable from this dump") says the `+use` grab controller runs at max speed 1000 u/s / max damp 2000 and lists Rust's `pickup.rs` MAX_SPEED 2500 as a P1 mismatch. gravgun.md (bin-srv constructor, hex constants, high) read the same grab-controller constructor in GMod's server binary as maxSpeed 2500 / maxDampSpeed 5000 and marks Rust's 2500 as a match. In the SDK the `+use` pickup and the physcannon share one grab-controller class, so the binary value applies to `+use` as well **[inference, combine step]**; by the lab rule the binary wins: Rust's 2500 is right and props G25 should be dropped (keep the 1 kg held mass and 12 u drop, which both agents agree on). The props row in the table carries the note.

C2. **Explosion screen shake.** player.md F13 (bin-server, high): the player's blast-damage handler in GMod only calls a Lua hook; the SDK's shake (amplitude 4, freq 1, 0.5 s, radius 1000) and ear-ring are not in the binary, so "explosion damage has no screen shake". hl2weapons.md and clientfx.md report a 25/150/1.0/750 shake for frag/SMG1 grenade explosions and 20/150/1.0/1250 for the combine ball. These are different mechanisms (the damage handler vs the explosion entities' own `UTIL_ScreenShake`), so both can hold; the table keeps the weapon-side shakes as gaps and the player-side "no shake" as a match. Within Rust, clientfx says a `Shake` event and `calc_shake` already exist (main.rs 4097-4110, 9105-9136) while hl2weapons lists the grenade shake as missing — i.e. the client can shake but the server does not emit it for those explosions **[inference]**.

C3. **Damage indicator.** player-gaps G26 (P0) says no damage indicator exists on the Rust client ("not found in main.rs"); ui-gaps E1/E2 cite `main.rs:1569-1600` (`damage_from`, HL2MP/CS dot > 0.3 model) and `6066-6072` (2/s decay) and `6683-6700` (30,100 / 36 wide / 240/200 tall geometry) as an existing but wrong implementation (P1). The ui reading is the more specific one; the gap is "wrong model and timing", not "absent". The two also quote different layout numbers for the game: player.md F87 gives the compiled client defaults `dmg_xpos 10, dmg_ypos 80, dmg_wide 30, dmg_tall1 300, dmg_tall2 240` (bin-client), ui.md §9.1 gives 30,100, 36 wide, 240/200 tall (HudLayout.res). Both can be true: the res file overrides the compiled defaults at runtime **[inference]**; implement the res values.

C4. **Landing / fall thresholds.** movement.md 8.1 reads the binary's comparison chain as the SDK multiplayer set 350 (punch) / 290 (0.85 step) / 580 (1.0 step + damage call) with the constants unresolved, and notes the repo's recordings (hurt at 526.5, punch at 342) contradict that set; it proposes the HL1-branch set 303 / 263.25 / 526.5 as GMod's likely values. player.md F26 (Lua high, SDK high) states the fall-damage threshold as 526.5 u/s (`GetFallDamage` default `(speed - 526.5) * 100/396`). Not strictly contradictory (the Lua formula zeroes below 526.5 whatever the engine threshold is), but the engine-side trigger is open; one landing at 290-350 u/s on srcds settles it.

C5. **Sandbox duck time.** movement.md: binary uses per-player DuckSpeed and `player_sandbox.lua` sets 0.1 s; the repo's `docs/spec/movement.md:50` measured 0.4 s. The agent attributes the recording to the base player class (bot without the sandbox class). Internal to movement but it changes a P0 fix; re-record with `Player:GetDuckSpeed()` logged.

C6. **Swim sound period.** movement.md: binary `Player.Swim` every 500 ms while jump is held under water; the repo measured 1000 ms (possibly engine-level sound throttling). Open.

C7. **Footstep volume coverage.** movement-gaps #10 marks footsteps "Yes (measured)" with only landing details missing; sound's summary marks "footstep volume ignores material" (`gm-move/src/lib.rs:1194-1197`) as P0. movement.md 10 does list the 0.25/0.55 and 0.4/0.7 surface values, so the sound agent's gap stands; movement simply did not compare per-material volumes. Table keeps it under sound.

C8. **Death ragdolls.** player-gaps G19 calls Rust's `spawn_death_ragdolls` a match "in substance"; props-gaps G12 lists the missing `g_ragdoll_maxcount` 8 LRU and 0.5 s fade. Complementary, not contradictory: player's row is about creation/force, props' about lifecycle.

C9. **Combine ball lifetime.** hl2weapons: `sk_weapon_ar2_alt_fire_duration` 4 s (SDK default assumed, not in dump) vs Rust 300 ticks (4.5 s) with a 267-tick stop; the agent itself says "keep measured if the harness says so". Open until the convar default is read.

C10. **Tracer frequency wording.** hl2weapons A5 "every 2nd bullet for SMG/AR2, every bullet for pistol-class"; clientfx 6.6 "1 in 2 for HL2 weapons" and open for the shotgun. Same fact, clientfx is less specific; use hl2weapons.
