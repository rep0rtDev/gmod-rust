# Server tick (`gm-server`)

One fixed step of 0.015 s composes the measured pieces: every player's usercmd goes through
`gm-move` against the BSP/displacement collision world, sandbox commands (`gm_spawn`, tool gun
clicks, `gmod_undo`, `gmod_cleanup`, `sbox_*`) go through `gm-sandbox`, and the props and
constraints the sandbox owns are mirrored into the Jolt world of `gm-physics`:

- a new sandbox prop becomes a dynamic body at its placement pose (PHY hulls, mass, surface,
  damping, drag areas and inertia of the model), frozen props are kinematic;
- a prop the tool gun moved (weld right click, axis) is teleported (`PhysObj:SetPos/SetAngles`,
  velocity cleared) and its frozen flag follows `EnableMotion`;
- weld -> Jolt fixed constraint, rope -> distance constraint between the recorded local points
  (`length + addlength`, rigid as min = max), axis -> hinge through `LPos1` along the axis towards
  `LocalAxis`, nocollide -> the pair is rejected in the Jolt contact validation (a prop dropped onto a no-collided frozen prop falls through it to the floor), re-enabled when the constraint goes; the world is a static
  body, so constraints to the world attach to it;
- undo and cleanup remove constraints first, then bodies;
- after the physics step every body's pose flows back into the sandbox, so the next trace or
  spawn sees the props where physics left them.

Nothing is networked yet; the harnesses drive the loop directly.

## Measured

The sandbox and tool gun recordings (`sandbox_spawn_basic`, `toolgun_weld_rope`, `toolgun_axis`)
replayed through the whole loop, 3 ticks of physics after every op (20 on the explicit waits)
with no poses taken from the recording: `server_spawn_basic` 16 ops within 0.55 u / 1.2 degrees
(a crate spawned 20 u above the floor falls and lands like the game's), `server_weld_rope` 20 ops
within 0.12 u / 0.25 degrees, `server_axis` 8 ops within 0.02 u / 0.06 degrees (frozen crates).

The physgun runs in the loop too (`Player::held`, `PlayerCmd::physgun`): the weapon fires with the
eye before the player's move of the tick (grab by the tool trace against the prop hulls, hold with
the shadow controller, release on attack up, freeze on attack2 also flags the sandbox prop), then
physics steps, then players move. The seven `physgun_*` recordings replayed as `server_physgun_*`
give the same numbers as the standalone physgun harness: 5 of 7 within tolerance (freeze 0.50 u,
off-centre 0.49 u, pitch steps 0.50 u, rotate 1.95 u / 4.45 degrees, yaw steps 0.10 u), walk
hold 3.0 u on the acceleration ticks and hold_crate 8.5 u only after the release landing (the open
contact response of `phys_*`).

## Player life cycle (measured, `player_lifecycle`)

Recorded on a real sandbox server with a bot killed four times (`Player:Kill`) and left to the gamemode:

- Spawn: health 100 (max 100), armour 0, model `models/player/kleiner.mdl`, hull (-16, -16, 0) to (16, 16, 72), 13 weapons (`gmod_camera`, `gmod_tool`, `weapon_357`, `weapon_ar2`, `weapon_crossbow`, `weapon_crowbar`, `weapon_frag`, `weapon_physcannon`, `weapon_physgun`, `weapon_pistol`, `weapon_rpg`, `weapon_shotgun`, `weapon_smg1`) with the crowbar out; `PlayerSelectSpawn`, `PlayerSpawn`, `PlayerLoadout`, `PlayerSetModel` fire in that order on the spawn tick.
- Spawn point: one of the map's `info_player_start` (33 on gm_construct) at random (observed sequence 13, 17, 12, 2, 17 of 33: repeats allowed, no visible order), the player placed 1 u above the entity's origin and settling onto the floor over the next ticks.
- Death: the same tick `Alive()` is false, health 0, move type 5, a ragdoll exists, `Deaths` +1; the weapons are still listed that tick (the active one reads `weapon_357` for that tick, a weapon switch artefact) and are gone the next tick; `PlayerDeathThink` starts the tick after.
- Respawn: exactly 135 ticks after the death tick for a bot (2.025 s at 66.67 Hz; deaths at 60, 300, 540, 780 -> spawns at 195, 435, 675, 915), without any input. Convars during the recording: `sbox_godmode 0`, `sbox_playershurtplayers 1`, `sbox_weapons 1`, `mp_falldamage 0`, `sv_gravity 600`.

`gm-server` reproduces this: `Player` carries `alive`, `health`, `armor`, `max_health`, `model`, `weapons`, `active_weapon`, `deaths`, `frags`, `ragdoll`; `Server::spawn_player` picks a spawn point with a deterministic xorshift (the harness only checks membership and the 1 u offset since the game's pick is random), `kill_player` records the death tick, `tick` strips the weapons the tick after and respawns at death + `RESPAWN_TICKS` (135); dead players neither move nor use the physgun. Harness `player_lifecycle`: 1100 ticks equal (alive, health, armour, deaths, ragdoll, weapon list), the four respawns within 0.07 u of a spawn point. Open: the ragdoll as a physics entity, damage (no damage sources yet), fall damage, the human player's respawn on `IN_ATTACK`.

## Damage (measured, `damage_*`)

Recorded with a bot dropped from heights and hit with `TakeDamageInfo`:

- Armour: a hit of `d` with armour `a > 0` takes `min(0.8 d, a)` from the armour and the rest from health (30 at armour 100: armour -24, health -6; 50 at armour 10: armour -10, health -40). `DMG_FALL` ignores armour (an 80.7 fall at armour 100 leaves it at 100); `DMG_BURN`, `DMG_BLAST`, `DMG_CLUB`, `DMG_BULLET` and generic do not.
- Health is whole: the fractional part of every hit is carried over in a per-player accumulator (0.4 then 1.6 takes 0 then 2) which survives `SetHealth` and respawns (on a fresh server a 12.5 fall takes 12; run after other hits carrying 0.68, it takes 13), so recordings must start from a fresh server or account for it.
- Death by a hit: health reads 0 that tick, `PlayerDeath`, ragdoll, respawn 135 ticks later like `Player:Kill`. Death by a fall keeps the negative health (-15, -42, -69 recorded) and the bot did not respawn by itself within the 200 ticks recorded.
- Fall damage (`DMG_FALL`, applied on the landing tick, speed = the downward speed at the start of that tick): nothing up to 522 u/s, damage from 540 u/s; `mp_falldamage 0`: 10 flat; `mp_falldamage 1`: `(v - 526.5) * 25/99` (540 -> 3.41, 693 -> 42.05, 1197 -> 169.32, all within 0.01).

`gm-server`: `damage_player(id, amount, type)` with the armour share, the carried fraction and the two death paths; `fall_damage_progressive` for `mp_falldamage`; the landing is detected from the movement state (`on_ground` false -> true). Harnesses `damage_fall`, `damage_hits`, `damage_fall_armor` equal every tick (health, armour, alive); `damage_fall_progressive` and `damage_fall_threshold` are equal since the ground probe of `categorize` was measured at 1.0-1.32 u with an up-only speed guard (see movement.md: the game grounds a 1089 u/s fall 0.3 u above the floor, which the old 2 u probe plus `|vz|` guard missed by a tick).

## Player ragdoll (measured, `ragdoll_still`)

On the dedicated server a dead player's body is an `hl2mp_ragdoll` entity created on the death tick at the player's origin with zero angles and **no physics objects** (`GetPhysicsObjectCount()` is 0 for all 135 ticks it exists): the ragdoll is simulated on each client from the player's model (`models/player/kleiner.phy`: 15 solids, 14 `ragdollconstraint` blocks and `collisionrules` pairs in the PHY text), the server only carries the record. `gm-server` keeps that record (`Ragdoll`: class, model, origin, angles, velocity, tick) on the dead player; harness `ragdoll_still` equal (class, count 0, tick, origin, alive per tick). The client-side ragdoll simulation (Jolt articulated body from the PHY constraints) is a client realm item.

## Weapons: loadout, ammo, switching

Recording `weapon_switch` (bot with the sandbox loadout, `SelectWeapon` at six ticks): the 13 weapons' clips, ammo types, slots, weights and hold types are in `gm_server::weapons::WEAPONS` (357 6/6, AR2 30/30 + AR2AltFire, crossbow 1/1, pistol 18/18, shotgun 6/6, SMG1 45/45 + SMG1_Grenade; the rest have no clip); reserve ammo on spawn in `SPAWN_AMMO` (357 32, AR2 100, AR2AltFire 6, Buckshot 64, Grenade 5, Pistol 256, SMG1 256, XBowBolt 32; RPG_Round and SMG1_Grenade 0). A switch is immediate: the selected weapon is active in the tick of the call, and both fire timers are set to the weapon's deploy time, then count down 0.015 per tick without clamping (crowbar 0.2333, physgun 0.2417, pistol 0.2, RPG 0.275, shotgun 0.1833, SMG1 0.2083; the others not measured yet). - .357: 0.75 from now per shot, no spread; deploy 0.25 (measured here, the switch recording had no .357).
- Pistol: a fresh press fires once 0.1 s passed since the last shot even while the 0.5 cycle runs (taps every other tick give a shot every 8 ticks), timer 0.5 from now; held, it fires when the cycle expires, the timer advancing from its expired value (0.475 / 0.48 reads); not during deploy or reload (`busy`). Empty clip: a press once the timer expired dry-fires (timer 0.4, gate 0.2, no bullet); with the button up it reloads. IN_RELOAD with a non-full clip, or the empty-clip reload: 1.4333 s, the clip fills from the reserve when it expires (18 after 17: reserve 256 -> 255; after 0: -> 238).

## Projectiles (measured, `fire_*_proj`; simulated in `projectile`)

Six recordings with the probe tracking every rocket, bolt, grenade and ball per tick (class, origin, velocity, angles, move type, removal tick and point). Rocket (`rpg_missile`, bot eye at (703.4, -513, -79) aiming +y): spawns in the press tick about 6 u right, 16.5-17.4 u forward and 1.2 u below the eye with velocity 300 along the aim plus (0, 0, 119), falls under 600 gravity in MOVETYPE_FLYGRAVITY (vz -9 per tick) for 20 ticks, then ignites: MOVETYPE_FLY at 1500 along the original aim and steers toward the aim point with a damped wobble (pitch error peaks 4.2 degrees at 9 ticks, back to 0.3 at 30), explodes on the wall at y 1392 (100 ticks) or on the sky (373 ticks for a -30 pitch shot), and the RPG then sets both timers to 1.6667. Bolt (`crossbow_bolt`): 3500 along the aim from the eye (52.5 u ahead on its first tick), MOVETYPE_FLYGRAVITY with gravity 0.05 (vz -0.45 per tick); off the wall at y 1410 (36 ticks) it deflects to (-145, 2371, 1117), 2610 u/s = 0.75 of 3500, under full gravity, and is removed on the sky 143 ticks later; the crossbow's clip and timers are untouched by the bolt's life. Grenade (`npc_grenade_frag`, VPHYSICS): leaves in the throw tick 8 u right, 34 u forward and 14 u above the eye at (0, 1117.7, 516.8) for a -20 pitch (1189 along the aim plus 110 up), air drag about -10 u/s per tick on 1100 u/s plus 600 gravity (vz -13.5 per tick), bounces on the floor at z -139..-144 from tick 110 after the throw, explodes 175 ticks (2.625 s) after the throw whatever the hold. Ball (`prop_combine_ball`, VPHYSICS, no gravity): 1000 along the aim from the eye (15 u ahead on its first tick), reflects off the wall at 126 ticks keeping 1000 u/s ((-54, 905, 421)), stops (MOVETYPE_NONE) at 267 ticks and is removed 300 ticks (4.5 s) after launch; the AR2's timers are untouched. Damage of the explosions and the bolt not measured yet.

Simulation (`gm_server::projectile`, `Server::tick_projectiles`; the weapon's `Inventory::launched` hands the launch to the server, removal of a rocket calls `Inventory::rocket_died`). Move types integrate gravity half before and half after the move (rocket z steps 114.5, 105.5, ... from vz 119, 110, ...). The bolt traces a 2 u box (it stops 6.4 u short on the grazing ramp), reflects at 0.75 under full gravity and is removed on a sky surface: `gm_collision::World::is_sky` finds the brush side the trace ended on and reads `SURF_SKY`/`SURF_SKY2D` from its texinfo. The ball is a 10 u sphere (its centre turns 10 u along the normal before the plane, 45 u early on the ramp) that reflects inside the step without losing speed. Four more rocket recordings with the laser dot tracked (`env_laserdot` sits on the owner's aim trace; `fire_rpg_proj_rep` is identical to `fire_rpg_proj`, the rocket is deterministic): after ignition the rocket turns as a damped second-order system in angles, `turn = 0.856 turn + 0.00887 (angles to dot - heading)` per tick (fit rms 0.013 deg/tick over four shots), and the first turn after ignition is a kick down and left of 0.50-0.91 pitch and 0.16-0.19 yaw that does not follow the error to the dot (it shrinks as the error grows: 0.91 at 0.26 deg error, 0.50 at 1.87); we use a fixed (0.75, 0.17), so rocket paths stay 5-12 u off and the flat shot hits the ramp 2 ticks late (open). Rocket steering, eight shots (`fire_rpg_proj*`: pitch 0/-10/-30/+5/+10, yaw 45/90/135, dot at 252-7252 u; the probe now records `GetLocalAngularVelocity`, which stays zero): the heading turn after ignition is a geometric sequence with ratio 0.86 per tick in every shot (0.954, 0.823, 0.699, ... deg), and its first term does not follow the angular error to the dot. Shots whose dot is near (252 and 329 u, error 1.5-1.9 deg) turn exactly onto the dot (the geometric sum lands 0.5-4 u from it), so the law is a blend of the heading toward a target direction at 14 % per tick. Shots whose dot is far turn toward a direction below and left of the aim although the error is 0.07 deg: 6.4-7.0 deg down and 1.1-1.3 deg left at 1900-7252 u, 4.7-4.9 deg down at 622-964 u (the implied point lies 44 u below the dot at 622 u, 213 at 1900, 830-880 at 7000), then steer back to the dot. The target direction the game blends toward is therefore not the direction to the dot from the rocket, and no origin (eye, muzzle, rocket) gives a direction that stays 7 deg below the aim as the dot recedes; open. The implementation keeps the fitted `turn = 0.856 turn + 0.00887 err` with a constant first kick, which is why the eight rocket harnesses are red (5-12 u).

The grenade is a rigid body in the server's Jolt world (`projectile::Physics`; model `models/weapons/w_npcnade.mdl`, PHY mass 1, volume 87.2, surfaceprop `grenade`, drag areas (1, 0.80, 1), no damping): thrown from 8 u right, 33.7 u ahead and 13.9 u above the eye (measured at pitch -20) at 1189 along the aim plus 110 up, fuse 175 ticks. Its orientation matters for the drag: the body spawns with angles (0, 0, 0) for an aim yaw of 90 (we use (0, yaw - 90, 0)), so its y axis (area 0.80) points along the flight, and rolls 600 deg/s about its x axis (+9 deg per tick in all three throws) plus a random pitch spin (487, -633, 200 deg/s measured; we draw uniform within 700 from the server's xorshift). With the x axis along the flight instead the drag was 18 % too strong and the grenade fell 25 u behind by the bounce; with the measured orientation it is 3.4 u behind at the game's bounce (tick 104) in `fire_frag_proj` and 2.2 u in `fire_frag_proj_rep`. The game's own two throws of the same shot differ by up to 8.5 u and 9.5 u/s before the bounce and diverge after it (x -96 vs -5 u/s at 147 ticks), so the frag harnesses compare within that envelope (`proj_pos` 9, `proj_vel` 10) up to the first bounce of either side, then the removal tick only. The drag is the crate's `k v^2` law scaled by the projected area: the fractional loss per tick over speed stays at 5.0-6.0e-4 per (u/s * s) from 1300 down to 400 u/s and correlates 0.6-0.8 with the angle between the body axis and the velocity. Harnesses: `fire_crossbow_proj`, `fire_ar2_proj`, `fire_frag_proj`, `fire_frag_proj_rep` equal (bolt and ball within 0.44 u per tick, the crossbow re-arms both timers the tick after its refill); the eight rocket harnesses red (steering target, above); `fire_frag_proj_hold` red: that throw's slow pitch spin (200 deg/s) keeps the game's grenade faster than our drawn spin allows (vy 1015 vs 1000 at 12 ticks, 26 u apart at the bounce) - the spin's random law is not measured beyond three throws.

## Projectile damage (measured, `hit_rpg_*`, `hit_crossbow`, `hit_ball`, `hit_frag_down`, `hit_smg1_grenade`)

Ten recordings of a bot firing a projectile weapon at a second bot 100-200 u ahead (1000 hp, the probe also tracks the projectiles and the target's velocity). Order within a tick: the player moves first, then the entities; a projectile's hit or blast pushes and hurts a target that has already moved, so the push shows raw in that tick's velocity and friction takes it from the next tick (bullets, resolved in the command phase, were measured the other way round: push visible after friction). A death in the entity phase respawns 134 ticks later, one less than a bullet death (`hit_ball`).

Explosions (`weapons::explosion`, `Server::explode`): the blast is 1 u above the inflictor's position. For every live player the distance is taken from the blast to the first point of the player's hull along the ray to the player's eye (origin + 64) - 0 when the blast is inside the hull - and the damage is `base * (1 - dist / radius)`: rocket 150 over 300 u (111.90 at 76.2 u, 86.48 at 127.1, 19.34 at 261.4 - radius 300.0-300.2 from all three), frag grenade 150 over 250 (49.26 at 168.0), SMG1 grenade 100 over 250 (98.19 at 4.6); the eye as the target point is what makes the radius constant (the hull's centre gives 316-324, the feet 290-312), and a blast whose ray to the eye is blocked by the world does nothing. A rocket that ran into the player deals the full 150 (`hit_rpg_floor20`: the rocket died 7 u inside the hull, 2.7 u above the floor). Armour takes its usual 80 % share (100 armour, 19.34 blast: health -4, armour 100 -> 84). Knockback: `5 * damage` u/s (damage before armour) from 10 u below the inflictor's position toward the player's centre (origin + 36), the vertical part capped at 250 (measured 250 twice, 134 and 111 uncapped); a grounded player keeps the ground and loses the vertical part the next tick (position settles 0.97 u lower). Direct hits: a bolt deals 100 as DMG_BULLET | DMG_NEVERGIB (4098) and stops 1 u before the hull (`projectile_touch`, swept); a combine ball kills (damage = the target's health, DMG_DISSOLVE | DMG_CRUSH) when its 10-u sphere overlaps the hull and flies on; a rocket collides with the player's hit boxes, not the hull (`hit_rpg_direct`: the game's rocket passed 2 u under the hull's top, over the head box, and exploded on the far wall), and the laser dot lands on the hit boxes too. Bullet damage types by ammo: pistol/SMG1 DMG_BULLET | DMG_NEVERGIB (4098), .357 DMG_BULLET | DMG_ALWAYSGIB (8194), buckshot DMG_BULLET | DMG_NEVERGIB | DMG_BUCKSHOT (536875010).

## Weapons on props (`shoot_prop_*`)

New probe kind `shoot_prop`: a bot fires one weapon at a `prop_physics` spawned `dist` ahead (crate `wood_crate001a`, health 30, mass 30; oil drum `oildrum001`, max health 1 / health 0 = unbreakable, mass 30, 44 u tall - aim at its centre, pitch 23 at 100 u). Facts from 22 recordings: bullets deal the single-player `sk_plr_dmg_*` values to props (pistol 5, .357 40, buckshot 8 per pellet, 7 pellets 56 as one event; against players the multiplayer table applies), damage types as on players without NEVERGIB (pistol/.357 2, buckshot 536870914). The damage force is a per-ammo constant along the shot (pistol 668, .357 10906, buckshot 1309 per pellet, crowbar 3000, bolt 21000, blasts as on players) and the prop's velocity changes by `force / mass`: .357 on the drum 334.6 u/s along the aim's horizontal, measured 327.7 (the vertical part goes into the floor), bolt 644 vs 634, pistol 20.5 vs 11.9 (light hits lose more to floor friction and the hit 28 u above the origin spins the drum). A frozen prop logs the hit and does not move. The crate takes 5 from a pistol (30 -> 27 shown, the health read truncated after armour-less float damage) and breaks (removed in the hit tick) from the .357's 40. The combine ball hits the drum nine times in a row (1000 each, type 67108865), the drum unharmed and thrown at 1540 u/s. The crowbar deals 10 DMG_CLUB with force 3000 (drum +63.5 u/s). ## Bullets on players

Recordings `hit_*` (a bot shoots a second bot standing 200 u ahead with 1000 health; every `EntityTakeDamage` / `ScalePlayerDamage` on the target, its health, armour and position per tick). Damage per bullet is the multiplayer ammo table, not the `sk_plr_dmg_*` convars the server also reports (pistol 12 vs sk 5, SMG1 12 vs 4, .357 75 vs 40, buckshot 4 per pellet vs 8): `weapons::bullet_damage`. The gamemode doubles head hits (12 -> 24, 75 -> 150), chest and stomach are unscaled, limbs unmeasured (a centre-line shot passes between the legs: no hit at knee or foot height, none above the head at 71 u). The hit group comes from the model's hit boxes: `gm_formats::mdl::hitboxes` reads the default set (kleiner: 17 boxes, head / spine / pelvis / arms / thighs / calves / feet / toes) and `weapons::HitModel` places them on the skeleton and traces the bullet against the boxes, the world blocking first. The skeleton must be in the game's standing pose, not the bind pose: `models/player/kleiner.mdl` carries a single sequence (`ragdoll`) and no include models, the player animations live in the gamemode's shared animation models (`models/m_anm.mdl` / `f_anm.mdl`: 56 ValveBiped bones, 464 sequences, 1563 animations, applied by bone name), and a standing player with a weapon plays `idle_<hold type>` (`idle_melee` for the crowbar, `idle_pistol`, ...; 30 frames at 30 fps, base idle `idle_all_01`). Their frame data sits in external animation blocks (`models/m_anm.ani`, `IDAG` v48; the header's block table triple at byte 0x15C, 247 blocks), and the idle animations are static poses: one 64-bit quaternion per bone (flags 0x20) plus a 48-bit position on the pelvis (0x21), no run-length streams - `gm_formats::mdl::{sequences, anim_descs, anim_blocks, static_pose}` and `weapons::sequence_pose` read them, `HitModel::posed` applies them as bone-name overrides. Facing differs between the two: kleiner's bind pose faces -y (`yaw_offset` 90), the animation model's root faces +x (0). With `idle_melee` the posed boxes reproduce every measured hit and miss: head 55.5-68 u (a shot at 71 misses, as in the game), the head's front 13.6 u ahead of the origin (the game's head hit lands 12.7 u ahead), chest front 11.4 (game 9), stomach 7.1 (game 5.1), the thighs leave a gap at the centre line so a knee or foot shot there passes between the legs; hit positions land within 1.1 u (head), 2.2 (chest), 1.6 (stomach) and 4-10 u on later hits after knock-back and spread. In the bind pose instead the head hit was 5.7 u off and the high shot hit. Without a hit model (`Server::hit_model` None) `weapons::hitgroup_at` falls back to height bands measured on kleiner (head >= 58 u, chest 44-58, stomach 26-44). Knock-back: a horizontal push along the aim of 4.18 u/s per point of scaled damage (24 -> 100.4, 12 -> 50.1, 150 -> 636), added after the target's friction and before its move, so the full push shows in the hit tick and decays by the walk friction (8, stop speed 10) afterwards; the .357 reads +1.5 % and the shotgun -2 % against the linear law (open). Spread is random in the game, so the harness compares the first hit of a spread weapon exactly and later hits only where the game's bullets landed; the shotgun's pellets only by hit tick. All ten `hit_*` harnesses equal (pistol, armour, chest, stomach, legs, feet, high, .357, SMG1, shotgun).
